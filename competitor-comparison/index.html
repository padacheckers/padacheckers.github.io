A 20-Minute Competitor Comparison with Authority Metrics

Small business owners are often told to "build authority" without being shown what that means for their own market. A quick comparison with a few real competitors makes it concrete. This exercise takes about twenty minutes and gives you a short, honest list of what to work on.

Step 1: Pick three real competitors (5 minutes)

Search for the two or three phrases a customer would actually type to find you, in your own city or niche. Note the sites that appear on the first page and are genuinely similar to you. Ignore directories and huge marketplaces; you are not competing with them directly. Three competitors is enough.

Step 2: Collect the same numbers for everyone (5 minutes)

For your site and each competitor, record domain authority, the page authority of the page that ranks, the spam score, and the domain age. Use the same tool for everyone, because scores from different vendors are not comparable. A bulk lookup such as DAPA Checker lets you paste all four domains at once.

Step 3: Read the gaps, not the totals (5 minutes)

  • Your DA is close to theirs but your page PA is much lower: the problem is the page, not the site. Improve that page and link to it from your other pages.
  • Your DA is far lower: you need more sites in your niche or area to mention and link to you. That is a longer project.
  • A competitor has a high spam score: do not copy where their links come from.
  • Everyone is low: good news. Content quality and local relevance will matter more than authority in your market.

Step 4: Write three actions (5 minutes)

Turn the gaps into exactly three things you can do this month, for example: rewrite the main service page, ask two local partners to list you on their sites, and claim the profiles that competitors have and you do not.

Repeat the same exercise every three months with the same competitors and the same tool. The trend over time is far more useful than any single score.
